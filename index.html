Ontario G1 Test Prep

Practise for the Ontario G1 driving knowledge test with practice questions and mock exams on your iPhone.

Disclaimer: Ontario G1 Test Prep is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Government of Ontario, the Ministry of Transportation of Ontario (MTO), or DriveTest.

Features

Free: Mock Exams 1–4 plus a preview (about 30%) of each Practice mode. Free users see Google AdMob banner ads, and the app asks permission through Apple's tracking prompt before personalised ads.

Premium: unlocks Mock Exams 5–10, all Practice modes and full Stats, and removes ads.

The app includes 10 mock exams of 20 questions each. Questions are original and based on publicly available Ontario driving rules. All content is bundled in the app, with no server and no accounts.

G1 Prep Premium – Premium Monthly: $6.99 per month (CAD in Canada, USD in the US), an auto-renewing subscription charged to your Apple ID. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, or use Manage Subscription in the app's Settings. Restore Purchases is available on the Premium screen and in Settings.
