Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

Ontario G1 Test Prep (“the App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores your quiz activity on your device only: exam attempts, the answers you select, questions you flag and your practice progress. This information is used to show your results and statistics and to build practice sessions. We do not receive it, and we cannot see it.

2. No account required

The App does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or home address.

3. Advertising

The free version of the App shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use certain information, such as your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to deliver and measure ads. If you allow tracking when the App asks, ads may be more relevant to you; if you decline, ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Learn more in Google’s advertising policy. Premium subscribers do not see ads.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Premium is an optional auto-renewing subscription handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment details. Apple tells the App whether your subscription is active so premium features can be unlocked.

5. Sharing and sale of information

We do not sell your personal information. The only third-party service that receives data from the App is Google AdMob, as described above, and Apple for subscription processing.

6. Children

The App is intended for people preparing for the G1 licence in Ontario and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Because your quiz data stays on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with a new effective date.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email chowchowbuy@gmail.com.
