Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open the app, tap the gear icon on the Home tab to open Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone open Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, choose Ontario G1 Test Prep and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also use Manage Subscription inside the app’s Settings once subscribed. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?

Your exam history and practice progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.

Are the questions official?

No. The questions are original practice questions written from the Official MTO Driver’s Handbook. The app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ontario Ministry of Transportation, and passing the practice exams does not guarantee passing the official test.
