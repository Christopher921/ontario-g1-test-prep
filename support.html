Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Premium purchase?

Open the app and use the "Restore Purchases" option on the Premium screen. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Why do I see ads?

The free version shows banner ads. Upgrading to Premium unlocks the full practice content.

Are the questions the same as on the real test?

No. The questions are practice material written to help you study. The official test may differ. Always refer to the official Ontario Driver's Handbook for the authoritative rules of the road.

How do I delete my data?

Your progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.
